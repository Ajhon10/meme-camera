## Policy release notes for the publisher

Supply the publisher's legal name and working support/privacy contact, finalize the effective date and applicable jurisdiction, and review actual distribution/data practices before presenting the policy drafts as final. No owner identity, contact address, or legal acceptance record has been invented. The in-app reader does not require users to agree to these drafts.

The privacy document reflects the code's local processing, optional persistent pose signatures, local effect images/settings/tutorial progress, and the separate transmission performed by meeting platforms. It does not claim that no personal information is processed simply because that processing is local.

Where Philippine law applies, the [National Privacy Commission's published Data Privacy Act](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/) provides the statutory context for transparency, applicable processing duties, and data-subject rights. Other jurisdictions and distribution arrangements may impose different requirements. Obtain appropriate review before public release; these documents are not a regulatory-compliance certification.
